# Gaming vs Academic Performance Analysis | Power BI

## Project Overview

This Power BI project explores the relationship between gaming habits and academic performance among students. The dashboard analyzes how gaming hours, gaming intensity, study habits, and gaming categories influence grades and overall academic outcomes.

The objective is to identify patterns, trends, and insights that help understand whether gaming positively or negatively impacts student performance.

---

## Objectives

* Analyze the impact of gaming hours on academic performance.
* Compare study habits and gaming behavior.
* Evaluate pass rates across different gaming intensity levels.
* Identify high-performing and low-performing gaming categories.
* Generate actionable insights from student behavioral data.

---

## Key Insights

* Students with balanced gaming and study habits tend to achieve better academic outcomes.
* Excessive gaming hours may negatively impact academic performance.
* Study time remains a strong predictor of higher grades.
* Gaming category preferences show varying academic performance patterns.
* Moderate gaming appears less harmful than high-intensity gaming.
